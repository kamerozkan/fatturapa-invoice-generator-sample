# Data Notice

## Purpose and provenance

This repository is a technical sample for the public
[FatturaPA Invoice Generator](https://apify.com/kamerozkan/fatturapa-invoice-generator) Actor.

The three outputs were produced on 2026-07-30 by the local release engine from
the paired synthetic inputs, then mapped into the production dataset-row shape.
Every generated artifact passed the complete pinned local validation stack:
the pinned FatturaPA FPR12 1.2.3 compatibility XML Schema.

The examples came from local fixture generation on July 30, 2026. During that fixture exercise no Actor was published, no network or tax platform received an invoice, no customer data was processed and no billing event occurred. The Actor is now publicly listed; the historical fixtures are still local examples. `sampleProvenance` is a repository-only annotation, not a field
normally added by the Actor.

## Synthetic invoice data

Names, postal addresses, email addresses, VAT or tax identifiers, registration
identifiers, electronic endpoints, purchase-order references, and bank account
values in the inputs are synthetic examples. `.test` email domains are reserved
for testing. Do not treat any identifier as belonging to a real organization or
person, and do not use the fixtures for payment or tax filing.

Real invoice inputs can contain personal, financial, contractual, tax, and
commercially confidential data. Users remain responsible for lawful processing,
access controls, retention, deletion, encryption, incident response, and all
applicable privacy, tax, accounting, and contractual duties.
